## Counterexample hunt

Searched for a fragile step: the per-k bound at `CMap.lean:1051` is
`|F_k - G_k| ≤ (1/2^M) · stuff_per_k(n,k)` where `stuff_per_k` already absorbs
the X-leg and Y-leg constants (`CMap.lean:783-784`, `bound_x_k + bound_y_k +
bound_αR_at_0 + bound_βR_at_0 + 6`). The triangle inequality assembly at
`CMap.lean:1244` therefore gives `(d n + 1)·bound_max(n)·(1/2^M)` — with **no
factor 2**. The LaTeX claim at `L4.tex:24` of a `· 2 ·` factor before `2^{-M}`
is therefore numerically *imprecise* (more generous than the actual Lean
estimate). However, it is not unsound: a tighter bound implies the looser one,
and `closeout_nat (D, Bm, m) : (D+1)·Bm·2^m ≤ 2^(m + (D+1) + Bm + 2)`
(`CMap.lean:477`) already provides the `+2` pad without needing the factor 2.
The mismatch is in the LaTeX over-estimate, not in any actual proof step.
