## Restatement
The artifact proves P-R Axiom 1 (Linear Forms) for the first concrete
`ComputabilityStructure ℝ (C([α,β],ℝ))` instance, under the polynomial-approximation
predicate `IsComputableSeqCMap`. Given computable function-sequences `x, y`, computable
scalar double-sequences `coefα, coefβ`, and a recursive `d`, it exhibits a witness
`⟨aS, dS, ·, ·, ·⟩` certifying that `s_n = Σ_{k=0}^{d(n)} (coefα(n,k)·x_k + coefβ(n,k)·y_k)`
is computable. The soundness-critical sub-goal C2 is the norm bound
`∀ n m, ‖s n − polyApproxCMap aS dS n m‖ ≤ 1/2^m` (lines 988–1249). The witness pads

`dS(n,m) = max_{k≤d n} max(dX(k,M), dY(k,M))`; the bound is closed by a per-`k` triangle
decomposition (`perk_bound`) summed over `Finset.range (d n + 1)` and an exact ℕ
close-out (`closeout_nat`).
