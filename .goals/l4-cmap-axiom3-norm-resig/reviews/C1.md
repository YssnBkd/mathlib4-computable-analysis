# Verdict

The lemma is a faithful rational-input specialization of Pour-El & Richards
Ch. 0 Proposition 1 (chapt0:246-272). Statement, quantifier order, bound form,
and conclusion shape (Def-5a) all match the verbatim text. The diagonalisation
`r'(n,k) := r(n, e(n,k))` is exactly the rational sub-construction P-R
themselves perform at chapt0:209-215 in the Def-5⇒Def-5a equivalence proof,
specialised to the case where the input is already rational so the factor-2
that appears in the general real-input Prop 1 (chapt0:268-269) does not arise.
Three minor non-blocking improvements listed below.

## A. Statement faithfulness

1. **Rational-input specialization soundness.** Sound. P-R's "computable
   double sequence of real numbers" (chapt0:248) immediately unpacks via
   Def 5a (chapt0:203) into a *triple* rational sequence `{r_{n,k,N}}` with
   `|r_{n,k,N} − x_{n,k}| ≤ 2⁻ᴺ`. Skipping the real-tier and taking the
   rational data directly drops one error term (we have `|r_{n,k} − x_n|`
   instead of `|r_{n,k,N} − x_n|` after triangle inequality), which is why
   our bound is `2⁻ᴺ` cleanly and not `2 · 2⁻ᴺ`. **Nothing is lost** — the
   rational form is the only data Prop 1's proof actually extracts (chapt0:
   260-263); the real-input form is recoverable from this lemma plus a
   Def-5a unfolder when `IsComputableDoubleSeqReal` lands. The §2.5
   docstring (file lines 113-125) correctly flags this.

2. **Quantifier order.** Matches. P-R Def 4 at chapt0:191-195: "there is a
   recursive function `e: ℕ × ℕ → ℕ` such that for all `n, N`: `k ≥ e(n,N)`
   implies `|x_{n,k} − x_n| ≤ 2⁻ᴺ`". Order is `∃ e, Computable e ∧ ∀ n N k,
   k ≥ e(n,N) → ...`. Lean signature: `{e} (he : Computable e) (hconv : ∀ n N
   k, k ≥ e(n,N) → ...)` — `∃ e` exposed as implicit `{e}`, `Computable e`
   as `he`, `∀ n N k` as `hconv`'s leading binders. Exact match. No drift.

3. **Bound form.** Clean. `(1 : ℝ) / 2 ^ N` with `N : ℕ` elaborates to
   `(1 : ℝ) / (2 : ℝ) ^ N` via `HPow ℝ ℕ ℝ` (monoid pow). No ℤ-pow / NNReal
   ambiguity. Numerically equals `2⁻ᴺ` for `N ∈ ℕ`. Matches the §2 `IsComputableSeqReal`
   bound form at file:109 verbatim, which is what the conclusion requires.

4. **Def 5 vs Def 5a.** Conclusion is Def-5a-shaped. The witness constructed
   at file:153 is `fun p => r (p.1, e p)` and the bound established at
   file:184-185 is `|r' (n, k) − x n| ≤ 1/2^k` (precision index *equals*
   approximant index). That is exactly Def 5a (chapt0:203-207). No accidental
   Def-5 leakage — there is no auxiliary modulus inside the witness, only the
   diagonalised pair.

5. **Modulus output form / subsequence trick.** Sound. File:185 calls
   `hconv n k (e (n, k)) le_rfl`: this instantiates `N := k`, `k_input := e(n,k)`,
   and discharges the side-condition `e(n,k) ≥ e(n,k)` by `le_rfl`. Result:
   `|r(n, e(n,k)) − x n| ≤ 1/2^k`. This is the rational-only mirror of the
   P-R substitution at chapt0:209-215 (the "we simply replace `{r_{n k}}` by
   the computable subsequence `r'_{n,k} = r_{n, e(n,k)}`" step in the Def-5⇒
   Def-5a converse). Quantifier order makes this work: because `k` of the
   Lean signature serves *both* as the new approximant index *and* the
   precision parameter `N`, the diagonal `k_input := e(n, k)` collapses the
   two-index data into one. Verified.

## B. Insights mined from chapt0:246-280

6. **Prop 2 / Cor 2a / Cor 2b relevance to A3.** None. Th. 7's proof at
   chapt0:1012 cites only Prop 1 ("this implies, by Proposition 1 in Section
   2"). Prop 2 ("if and only if" version requiring monotonicity) is for the
   *converse* direction (extracting effective convergence from monotone
   computability) — A3 already has the effective modulus `e(n,N) = M · d(n,N)`
   delivered by the effective uniform continuity hypothesis, so the harder
   "only if" half of Prop 2 is not needed. Cor 2b (non-effective limit of
   computable sequence is non-computable) is a *counterexample* signpost,
   irrelevant to the positive direction A3 needs. **Phase 3 does not need to
   state or prove Prop 2 / Cor 2a / 2b.**

7. **Uniform vs effective convention.** Important pin. The convention at
   chapt0:220-223 makes "uniformly in `n`" mean *independent of `n`* and
   "effectively in `n`" mean *governed by a recursive `f(n)`*. Th. 7 uses
   `e(n,N) = M · d(n,N)` (chapt0:1003) where `d(n,N)` depends on `n` — so
   A3 is *effective* in `n`, not uniform. The current lemma signature
   `e : ℕ × ℕ → ℕ` is correctly the *effective* form. A uniform-in-`n`
   corollary (`e : ℕ → ℕ`) is trivially subsumed (instantiate `e (n, N) :=
   e' N`) and not separately needed for A3. Worth a one-line `-- ref:
   chapt0:220-223` in the docstring to make the choice explicit.

8. **Example 1 sanity.** `e(n, N) = (n + 1) · 2^N` from chapt0:232 fits the
   signature shape `ℕ × ℕ → ℕ`. Constructing it as `Computable` in Lean
   touches a known PITFALLS.md §1 obstruction: `Primrec.nat_pow` does
   **not** exist by that name in Mathlib; `(n+1) · 2^N` must be built via
   `Primrec.nat_rec` over `N` (or `(fun N => 2^N)` proved computable inline).
   Not a problem for our lemma (which takes `e` as input), but it is a
   guaranteed tripping point in **Phase 3** when A3 needs to construct
   `M · d(n, N)` Computably. The pattern surfaces twice — `M` (likely the
   `Nat.ceil` of `(β - α)`) and the constant `2`-factor are both involved.
   **Recommend** Phase 3 either pre-builds `Computable (fun (n, N) => 2 ^ N)`
   as a helper or sources `d(n, N)` from the effective-continuity hypothesis
   already in a Primrec-closed form. This is a workflow flag, not a flaw in
   C1.

## Recommended action (non-blocking)

Verdict is **sound**; A3 work should proceed to Phase 2 (C3) without
modification to C1. Three minor additions worth folding in opportunistically:

1. **Add one docstring sentence** at file:113-125 noting that the rational-input
   specialization yields `≤ 2⁻ᴺ` (not P-R's `≤ 2 · 2⁻ᴺ` at chapt0:268-269)
   because the rational-approximant-of-real error term collapses. This makes
   the bound-shape choice visible to future readers wondering why our lemma
   looks cleaner than the verbatim Prop 1 statement.

2. **Add `-- ref: chapt0:220-223`** near `e : ℕ × ℕ → ℕ` in the lemma
   header (or in the §2.5 introduction docstring) to pin the
   "effective"-not-"uniform" convention. Currently implicit.

3. **Flag for Phase 3 (C5)**: when building the A3 effective modulus
   `e(n, N) := M · d(n, N)` (chapt0:1003), `M` is computable as a constant
   `Nat.ceil (β - α) + 1` derived from `hα_c, hβ_c, hβ_le` — straightforward.
   `d(n, N)` comes packaged as `Computable` from the effective-continuity
   data of `{f_n}` (via the L4 `IsComputableSeqCMap` predicate). Their
   product should compose via `Primrec.nat_mul.to_comp.comp` cleanly. **No
   `Primrec.nat_pow` is needed** for A3's `e` because the `2^N` factor of
   chapt0:1003 is hidden *inside* `d(n, N)` (P-R's `d` outputs a `ℕ` like
   `2^N · L` for `L` = Lipschitz constant; the `2^N` is `d`'s output, not a
   separate exponentiation we have to construct). Confirming this on the
   Lean side will save Phase 3 an iter.
