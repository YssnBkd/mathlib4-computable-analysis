\begin{theorem}
  \label{thm:l4_cmap_instance}
  \lean{ComputableAnalysis.L4.computabilityStructureCMap_of}
  \uses{def:l3_computabilityStructure}
  $C([a,b], \mathbb{R})$ with sup-norm carries a \texttt{ComputabilityStructure}~$\mathbb{R}$ structure via the polynomial-approximation predicate, parameterized over an explicit rational bound $B$ on $\max(|a|,|b|)$ (matching P-R Ch.\,2:128's restriction to recursive reals).

  \textbf{Formalization state (2026-06-03, partial).} The witness $\langle aS, dS \rangle$ for \texttt{axiom\_linearity} is constructed and Computable; the \texttt{IsComputableSeqRat (flatten aS)} proof is closed (\textasciitilde 150 lines via \texttt{FinsetSumHelper.finsetSum\_rat} + helpers). The polynomial-approximant norm bound $\|s_n - \texttt{polyApproxCMap}\ aS\ dS\ n\ m\| \le 1/2^m$ is the sole remaining \texttt{sorry} in the body. \texttt{axiom\_limits} (= P-R Ch.\,0 Thm.\,4) and \texttt{axiom\_norms} (= P-R Ch.\,0 Thm.\,7) remain stub. P-R Ch.\,2:141--153.
  See \texttt{ComputableAnalysis/L4/Instances/CMap.lean:439}.
\end{theorem}
